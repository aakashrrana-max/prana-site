Planning your full-arch day around work

The first visit: one evening or a Saturday morning

Your free first visit covers an exam, a 3D scan and a digital smile preview. It doesn't require a day off, so many Brickell patients book it after work or on a Saturday.

Treatment day: block the day and the next few

Treatment day usually means removing teeth that can't be kept, placing the implants and, in many cases, attaching a fixed temporary set. With oral sedation, you won't be in shape to answer email that afternoon. A Thursday or Friday appointment lets the weekend absorb the first days of recovery, and some people add a day or two of remote work after that.

Back at your desk

Speaking with new teeth can take a little practice, so you may sound slightly different on calls for a short while. Swelling varies from person to person. If you have a presentation or a client event coming up, mention it at the first visit so the dates can be planned around it.

Healing and final teeth

Over the next few months the implants bond with the bone, depending on how you heal. Check-ins are short and can land early in the morning or in the evening, and the final arch is made in our in-house lab before it's fitted.
