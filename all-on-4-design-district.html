When the worry is looking fake

People who work around luxury retail, design showrooms, galleries and ICA Miami spend their days judging proportion, color and finish. So it's no surprise that the most common concern about full-arch teeth here isn't the procedure. It's whether the result will look like a row of identical white blocks.

Natural-looking teeth come from planning details: tooth length and width that suit your face, a shade that fits your skin tone and age, slight variation between teeth, and a gumline that sits where your lips expect it. Those choices start at the first visit, not at the end.

Designing a full arch that looks natural

Start with the smile preview

The free first visit includes an exam, a 3D scan and a digital smile preview. The preview lets you react to shape, length and overall look early, while changes are easy, and bring up anything you want different.

A temporary set that comes first

In many cases, a fixed temporary arch is attached on treatment day. It's designed to be functional while you heal, and the final arch is usually refined from there. Living with the temporary is a useful test: you'll notice what you like and what you'd change.

Final teeth made in our own lab

After a few months of healing, depending on how you heal, your final arch is made in our in-house lab. Final full-arch bridges are commonly made from materials such as zirconia or acrylic, and Dr. Rana explains how each looks and wears so you can choose. Working with an in-house lab makes it simpler to adjust details like shade or shape before the final fit.

Is All-on-4 the right approach?

All-on-4 places four implants in an arch to hold a fixed bridge of teeth. It's usually considered when most teeth in an arch are missing or failing, or for someone ready to leave a removable denture behind. Bone, gums and general health decide whether it's possible for you. Some cases need more implants for support, and then Dr. Rana may recommend All-on-X, which also starts at $9,999 per arch. If you only need one tooth replaced, see dental implants near the Design District, or, for teeth that are healthy but worn, veneers near the Design District.

Questions, answered

Will All-on-4 teeth look natural enough for client-facing work in the Design District?

For many people, yes, when the arch is planned around their face, lips and skin tone. The digital smile preview shows the proposed design early, and the final teeth are made in our in-house lab, where details can be adjusted. Results depend on your bone, gums and the material chosen.

Can I see what my new teeth will look like before treatment?

Yes. The free first visit includes a digital smile preview based on your 3D scan. It shows the planned shape and look so you can ask for changes before anything is made.

Is the temporary arch what my final teeth will look like?

Not exactly. The temporary set, which in many cases is attached on treatment day, is meant to get you through healing. Your final arch is made after healing and is where shade, shape and finish are refined.
