Full-arch care for people whose day is downtown

Downtown workdays are structured: county offices, courtrooms, law firms and corporate floors where a long lunch is hard to justify. If you've been living with a partial denture, a bridge that keeps coming loose or several teeth that hurt when you chew, it's easy to keep pushing the fix to next quarter. The All-on-4 approach replaces a whole arch with fixed teeth on four implants, so a mouthful of separate problems becomes one planned treatment.

Because the office sits on the same boulevard that runs through downtown, the first visit can happen before you head in, after you leave, or on a Saturday. Hours are Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm, beginning Monday, November 16, 2026.

Getting here from Downtown Miami

Drive north on Biscayne Blvd from Bayfront Park, Bayside Marketplace or the Kaseya Center. You'll pass the Arsht Center around NE 13th Street, and the office is at 3050 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 703, at NE 30th Street. Citi Bike Miami stations are another option around the city for the first visit.

Brightline riders: treatment day is not a train day

If you commute into MiamiCentral, the free first visit is easy to add to an office day, since a rideshare from the station area up Biscayne is quick. Treatment day is different. After oral sedation you shouldn't travel alone at all, and that includes the train ride home. Plan for an adult to pick you up at the office by car and drive you all the way home, even if that's well up the coast.

If home is far, talk with Dr. Rana about the timing of your follow-up visits too. Healing check-ins are short, and they can often be lined up with the days you're already in the city for work.

Who tends to be a good fit, and when All-on-X makes more sense

Full-arch treatment is usually considered when most teeth in an arch are missing or failing, or when a removable denture no longer works for you. Your bone, gum health and medical history shape the plan. Some people need more than four implants to support an arch, and for them Dr. Rana may suggest All-on-X, which starts at the same $9,999 per arch. When only one or two teeth are missing, single dental implants near Downtown Miami may be the better path.

Planning and treatment both stay with Dr. Rana, an NYU College of Dentistry-trained general dentist, working alongside our in-house lab. Cases that call for an oral surgeon are referred out.

From first visit to final teeth

  • Free first visit: an exam, a 3D scan and a digital smile preview of your proposed new teeth.
  • A written, itemized price before anything is scheduled.
  • Treatment day: failing teeth removed, implants placed, and in many cases a fixed temporary arch attached the same day, with oral sedation available.
  • A few months of healing while the implants bond with the bone, depending on how you heal.
  • Your final arch, made in our in-house lab and fitted when you're ready.
