For people who have worn dentures for years

Many long-time denture wearers ask whether it's too late for implants. Not necessarily. After teeth are lost, the jawbone tends to shrink over time, which is one reason an old denture stops fitting well. All-on-4 places four implants, often angled to use the bone that remains, to hold a fixed arch that stays in your mouth. How much bone you have, your gum health and conditions like diabetes all affect whether it's possible, and the scan helps answer that.

When four implants aren't enough support, Dr. Rana may suggest All-on-X, which adds implants and also starts at $9,999 per arch. If only a tooth or two is missing, look at dental implants for Little Havana. Dr. Rana, who trained as a general dentist at NYU College of Dentistry, plans and treats every case himself and refers to an oral surgeon when a case needs one.

What happens at each visit

  • Free first visit: an exam, a 3D scan and a digital smile preview that shows the family what the new teeth could look like.
  • Written price: the exact cost, itemized on paper, before anything is scheduled.
  • Treatment day: any teeth beyond saving come out, the implants go in, and often a fixed temporary arch is attached that same day. If nerves are a concern, oral sedation can help.
  • Healing: a few months for the implants to bond with the bone, depending on how each person heals.
  • Final teeth: made in our in-house lab and fitted when healing allows.

Cost, coverage and paying over time

A full arch at Prana starts at $9,999. In Miami, this kind of treatment is commonly quoted around $20,000 to $30,000 per arch, so families comparing offices should ask for an itemized quote listing implants, abutments, final teeth, extractions, sedation and the provisional set. Our All-on-4 cost guide helps with that.

Coverage questions come up often. Original Medicare generally doesn't cover dental implants, some Medicare Advantage plans include dental benefits, and Florida Medicaid dental coverage for adults is limited and generally doesn't include implants. Most major dental insurance is accepted, but plans with annual maximums often in the $1,000 to $2,000 range may cover only part of the work, and in every case we check your benefits for you.

Families often ask about paying over time: CareCredit, Cherry and LendingClub offer 0% financing on approved credit, and payments can begin at about $167 each month on approved credit. See financing.

The drive home to Little Havana

After oral sedation, an adult must drive the patient home, and on this route that's a 15 to 25 minute trip. The family member who came to the first visit is often the natural choice. Questions, answered

Can my daughter or son come into the All-on-4 consult with me?

Yes. Family members are welcome at the first visit to listen, ask questions and help with decisions. Many Little Havana patients bring the person who will drive them on treatment day.

¿Hablan español para la consulta de All-on-4?

Sí. Our team is bilingual in English and Spanish, and the whole consult can be in Spanish. You can also read about the treatment at our Spanish All-on-4 page.

I've worn dentures for 20 years. Is All-on-4 still possible?

It may be. Bone often shrinks after years without teeth, but the 3D scan shows what's there and whether four implants, more implants or another option makes sense. Dr. Rana explains the findings plainly before you decide.

Does Florida Medicaid pay for All-on-4?

For adults, Florida Medicaid offers limited dental coverage that generally excludes implants. Some Medicare Advantage plans and private dental plans may help with part of the cost. We check your benefits for you, and financing is available on approved credit.

Usually about 15 to 25 minutes, depending on traffic, via I-95 north to I-195 east and Biscayne Blvd, or SW 8th Street east and then north on Biscayne. The office has building parking and street parking nearby.
