Is a full arch the right call?

All-on-4 uses four implants per arch to hold a fixed set of teeth that you don't take out. It's usually considered when most teeth in an arch are missing or failing, or when someone wants a fixed alternative to dentures. Your bone, gums and overall health decide whether it's possible, which is why nothing is recommended before an exam and scan.

If your jaw needs more support, Dr. Rana may suggest All-on-X, which adds implants and starts at the same $9,999 per arch. If you're dealing with just one or two problem teeth, dental implants near Midtown may cover it. Dr. Rana is a general dentist who trained at NYU College of Dentistry, plans and treats each case himself, and refers to an oral surgeon when that's the safer route.
