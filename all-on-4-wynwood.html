What a full arch on implants actually is

With All-on-4, four implants are placed in the jaw to hold a fixed bridge of teeth across a whole upper or lower arch. It stays in; you brush and clean it rather than taking it out at night. It's usually considered when most of the teeth in an arch are missing or can't be saved. Whether it's right for you depends on your bone, gum health and general health.

Some people need extra implants for support. Dr. Rana may then recommend All-on-X, which starts from $9,999 per arch too. The steps, one at a time

  • Free first visit: exam, 3D scan and a digital smile preview that shows what your new teeth could look like.
