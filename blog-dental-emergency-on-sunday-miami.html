Dental emergency on a Sunday in Miami?
How to get safely to Monday morning.

By Dr. Aakash Rana, DDS · Updated October 2026

A dental emergency on a Sunday in Miami usually comes down to one question: can this be managed safely at home until a dental office opens on Monday, or does it need an emergency room today? Prana Dental is closed on Sundays, so this guide is about the hours in between. It explains how to sort your problem, what helps from Saturday night to Monday, when the ER is the right place, and how to reach us so Monday morning moves quickly.

Short answer: most toothaches, chipped teeth, lost fillings and loose crowns can be handled at home until Monday with warm-water rinses, a cold compress and an over-the-counter pain reliever taken as the label directs. Swelling spreading toward the eye or neck, fever, trouble breathing or swallowing, bleeding that won't stop, or an injury to the face means the ER now. A knocked-out adult tooth can't wait either.

Can it wait until Monday? A quick sorting guide

Decide by your symptoms, not by how much it hurts. Tooth pain can be miserable and still be safe to manage for a day. What makes a weekend dental problem truly urgent is a sign that an infection is spreading, that breathing or swallowing is affected, or that an injury goes beyond the teeth.

Saturday night to Monday: what helps

Many Miami pharmacies are open on Sunday, and a pharmacist can help you choose a pain reliever that is safe with your other medicines.

A toothache

  • Rinse with warm water, then floss gently on both sides of the sore tooth in case food is wedged there.
  • Hold a cold compress on the outside of your cheek for 15 to 20 minutes at a time. Skip heating pads.
  • Take an over-the-counter pain reliever exactly as the label says, if it is safe for you. Never press aspirin or any tablet against the gum, because it can burn the tissue.
  • Prop your head up on an extra pillow at night, and avoid very cold, very hot or sugary food and drinks.

A broken tooth, lost filling or loose crown

  • Keep any broken piece or the crown in a clean, closed container and bring it Monday.
  • Cover a sharp edge or an open hole with dental wax or temporary filling material from the pharmacy.
  • If a crown slides on easily, pharmacy temporary cement can hold it until Monday. Never use household glue. Our post on a lost crown or filling has more detail.
  • Chew on the other side for the rest of the weekend.

A sore bump on the gum

  • Rinse gently with warm salt water a few times a day, and don't squeeze or pop it.
  • Take a photo on Saturday night and again on Sunday morning. If the swelling is clearly growing, don't wait for Monday.
  • A bump like this is usually a sign of infection, so it needs a dentist even if it stops hurting. Our dental abscess treatment page explains why.

ER or urgent care: which one on a Sunday?

Go to the emergency room, or call 911, for the danger signs in the table: swelling spreading toward the eye or neck, a fever with a swollen face, trouble breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding or facial trauma. Those can mean an infection is moving into deeper spaces of the head and neck, or that a bone is broken, and they need hospital care.

An urgent care clinic can be a middle option for a fever or a mild swelling when you aren't sure what is going on, though many clinics are not set up for teeth. Neither an ER nor an urgent care clinic can usually repair a tooth. They can ease pain and, when it is appropriate, start antibiotics, so plan a dental visit afterward anyway.

Sunday dental emergencies: common questions

Are there dentists open on Sunday in Miami?

Some practices keep weekend hours, so search and call ahead. Prana Dental is open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm, and closed on Sunday. With danger signs such as spreading swelling, fever or trouble swallowing, go to the ER rather than searching.

Should I go to the ER for a toothache on a Sunday?

For a toothache on its own, usually not, because the ER can manage pain but generally can't fix the tooth. Go if the toothache comes with facial swelling and fever, trouble breathing or swallowing, or swelling moving toward your eye or neck. Otherwise, manage the pain at home and see a dentist Monday.

Can a tooth infection wait until Monday?

A mild one without swelling or fever often can, as long as you watch it closely. Infections can spread, so if the swelling grows, moves toward the eye or neck, or you get a fever or trouble swallowing, go to the ER without waiting. Even if it settles, it still needs treatment.

What can I buy at a pharmacy for a broken tooth on a Sunday?

Dental wax or temporary filling material to cover a sharp edge, a cold pack, and an over-the-counter pain reliever. Ask the pharmacist which pain reliever is safe with your other medicines and follow the label. Then call or text a dentist for Monday.

Can I text Prana Dental on a Sunday?

Yes. You can text or WhatsApp (786) 579-0521 any day, and messages sent on Sunday are answered after we open Monday morning. Don't rely on a message for anything urgent: for danger signs, go to the ER.
