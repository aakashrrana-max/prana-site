Dental implants cost in Miami vary widely depending on your case and who you ask. At Prana Dental, a single implant with the crown included starts at $1,850, and every patient gets a written, itemized price before treatment begins. Here's what actually drives the number, so you can compare quotes with confidence.

How much does a single dental implant cost in Miami?

At Prana Dental, a single implant with the crown starts at $1,850. That starting price covers the straightforward case: enough healthy bone, no extraction needed first, and no additional sedation. Your exact price is confirmed in writing after a free consult that includes a 3D scan, because every mouth is different and a fair quote has to be based on your actual X-rays, not a guess.

What actually drives the price up or down?

Implant pricing swings from patient to patient for real, specific reasons — not because practices are pulling numbers out of the air. The most common factors are:

  • Bone graft: if the jawbone at the implant site has thinned, a graft is often needed to give the implant a stable foundation before or during placement.
  • Extraction: if the tooth being replaced is still present and needs to come out first, that's an additional step beyond just placing the implant.
  • 3D imaging: a 3D scan helps plan exact implant position and depth relative to nerves and sinuses, and is standard for a safe, accurate placement.
  • Sedation: patients who are anxious or having more extensive work done sometimes choose oral sedation, which is available at Prana and priced separately from the implant itself.
  • How many teeth are missing: replacing one tooth is a different case than replacing several, which is where options like All-on-4 for a full arch often become more cost-effective than multiple single implants.

What does the $1,850 starting price actually include?

The starting price is for the implant itself and the crown that sits on top of it — the two pieces that make a "tooth" again, not just a partial procedure. Anything your case needs beyond that, like a graft or extraction, is added and disclosed before you agree to treatment, never after. You can see how implants compare to our other services on the dental implants page and full starting prices on our pricing page.

This matters because a lot of "starting at" prices online turn out to be for the implant post alone, with the crown, abutment, or imaging billed separately later. Ask any practice you're comparing what their advertised price actually covers before you assume it matches ours line for line.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

We accept most major dental insurance plans, and many plans do cover a portion of implant treatment, though coverage varies a lot by plan and by whether it's considered medically necessary. The most reliable way to know your out-of-pocket number is to bring your insurance information to your free consult, where we can check your specific plan and build a written plan around it.

What if I'm missing most or all of my teeth?

If you're missing several teeth or an entire arch, pricing per single implant isn't usually the right comparison anymore. All-on-4 replaces a full arch of teeth on four implants and starts at $9,999 per arch at Prana — often a better value than paying for many individual implants.
