dental implants in wynwood: what to know before you book

By Dr. Aakash Rana, DDS · Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: Single dental implants at Prana start at $1,850, with full-arch All-on-4 from $9,999 per arch, backed by a 5-year workmanship guarantee. Prana is just a short trip from Wynwood in neighboring Edgewater.

If you're missing a tooth or several and have been putting off looking into implants, Wynwood residents have a genuinely close option right next door in Edgewater, rather than a trek across the city. Before booking anything, here's what the implant process actually involves from start to finish, what it costs, and what to expect from the first visit so there are no surprises later, whether about the timeline, the cost, or what recovery actually feels like.

Who's typically a candidate for implants

Dental implants are generally an option for adults missing one or more teeth who have enough healthy bone to support the implant, or who can have that assessed and addressed as part of planning before moving forward. A single missing tooth, several missing teeth, or a full arch of failing or missing teeth are all situations implants can address — the right approach depends on your specific case, which is determined during an exam rather than assumed beforehand based on age or general health alone. Missing teeth for any reason at all — decay, injury, or a tooth that simply never came in properly — can all be reasonable starting points for the same basic conversation with your dentist.

Single implant vs. full-arch options

A single implant replaces one tooth with a titanium post topped by a crown, starting from $1,850 at Prana. For patients missing most or all of their teeth in an arch, All-on-4 uses four strategically placed implants to support a full replacement arch, starting from $9,999 per arch. Which option fits depends on how many teeth are missing, their location in the mouth, and the condition of the surrounding teeth and bone, something your dentist evaluates directly during your exam rather than guessing from your description alone over the phone. That's part of why imaging is such a core part of the very first visit.

What the process actually looks like

It starts with a free consult that includes an exam and imaging — a 3D scan or CBCT scan, depending on what your case needs — to assess bone volume and plan implant position precisely before any surgery is scheduled. From there, the implant is surgically placed, followed by a healing period during which the bone integrates with the implant, before the final restoration is attached on top. Because Prana mills crowns in-house, the final restoration for a single implant can often be completed without the added wait of an outside lab shipping a case back and forth over several weeks after healing is already complete.

Why bone volume gets checked so carefully

An implant only succeeds long-term if there's enough healthy bone to anchor it securely, which is why imaging is central to planning rather than an afterthought tacked on before surgery. If bone volume is limited in a specific spot, your dentist will explain what that means for your options during the consult, rather than moving forward with a plan that skips this step entirely. This is also where a CBCT scan's 3D detail matters most for accurate, safe placement, since guessing at bone volume simply isn't part of a responsible plan.

Cost, guarantee, and financing

Beyond the starting prices above, your exact cost depends on your specific case and any additional preparation needed before the implant itself can be placed, which is why an exam always comes before a firm number rather than the other way around. What's consistent is the 5-year workmanship guarantee on implant and full-arch work, and 0% financing from $167 a month on approved credit if you'd rather spread out the cost — checking your rate doesn't affect your credit score, so there's genuinely no downside at all to looking into it before you've decided anything at all.
