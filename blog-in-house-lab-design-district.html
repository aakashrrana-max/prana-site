why design district patients choose an in-house dental lab

By Dr. Aakash Rana, DDS · Updated September 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: Prana designs and mills crowns, veneers, and full-arch restorations in its own on-site lab rather than outsourcing — a level of direct control over the finished result that fits how Design District patients evaluate craftsmanship elsewhere.

If you spend time in the Design District, you're probably used to caring about who actually makes the thing you're paying for, not just the name on the storefront or the marketing around it. It's a reasonable standard to apply to dental work too, even though it isn't usually framed that way — and it's exactly where having an in-house lab makes a real, tangible difference in the final result you end up with in the mirror.

What "in-house" actually means here

Most dental offices send crowns and veneers to an outside laboratory company to be designed and manufactured, based on a written prescription and a scan file — a process with no direct oversight at all from the dentist who actually examined you and knows your bite and gum tissue firsthand. Prana keeps that entire process in-house instead: the same team that scans your teeth also designs the restoration digitally and mills it on-site from ceramic material, with the dentist reviewing the piece before it's placed in your mouth. It's a small operational difference on paper that ends up shaping the entire experience of getting a restoration made.

Why that control matters for the final result

When design and fabrication happen under one roof, there's no gap between the person who understands your bite and gum tissue and the person shaping the restoration to fit them. Small refinements — an edge that needs smoothing, a contact point that needs adjusting, a shade that's slightly off — get caught and corrected on-site rather than requiring a case to be shipped back to a separate company and remade from a distance without ever seeing you in person. It's the difference between overseeing a finished piece yourself and approving something made off-site, sight unseen, based only on a written description someone else filled out on your behalf.

The practical upside: speed and cost

Cutting out a separate outside lab also removes that lab's fee entirely from the cost structure, which is part of why porcelain crowns and porcelain veneers are priced at $999 each rather than a number that quietly climbs once outsourcing fees get factored in later on down the line. It also means many crown cases can be completed the same day instead of requiring a temporary and a second visit two weeks later — a meaningful difference if your schedule doesn't have room for an extra appointment squeezed between meetings or gallery hours. It's the kind of efficiency you'd expect from any well-run process, applied here to a crown instead of a project.

What's backed by a guarantee

Full-arch restorations and implant work done through the in-house lab are covered by a 5-year workmanship guarantee — a direct benefit of one team being accountable for design, fabrication, and fit from start to finish, rather than the dentist and the lab pointing to each other if something needs revisiting later on down the road. That accountability is easy to promise and much harder to actually deliver once two separate businesses are involved.

A process built to be seen, not just trusted

Because the lab is on-site rather than an invisible step happening somewhere else entirely, the process is naturally more visible — you can ask to understand how your specific restoration is being designed and what it's being made from, rather than simply being told to trust a black-box process you have no real window into. For patients who care about knowing exactly what they're paying for and how it's made, that visibility is itself part of the value being offered — the same basic instinct that draws people toward open kitchens, visible workshops, and working studios elsewhere in everyday life, rather than closed doors and finished products alone with no explanation.

Getting there from the Design District

Prana is just a short, easy drive from the Design District over in Edgewater at 3050 Biscayne Blvd, open Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM, with enough flexibility for an appointment on either end of a workday, or on a Saturday if that fits your schedule better. A free consult — including an exam and 3D scan — is the easiest way to see the in-house process in person before deciding on any treatment; you can book one whenever it fits your schedule, and ask as many detailed, specific questions about the process as you'd genuinely like to along the way, no matter how technical those questions get.
