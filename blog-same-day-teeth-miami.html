Same-day teeth in Miami: how immediate-load dental implants work

By Dr. Aakash Rana, DDS · Updated September 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: With immediate-load implants, the technique used in All-on-4, you can leave your surgery appointment with a fixed, non-removable temporary bridge instead of a gap or a removable denture, as long as your bone and implant stability meet certain conditions checked beforehand.

"Same-day teeth" is accurate, but it's often misunderstood. Here's exactly what happens on the day of surgery, what you actually leave with, and when the permanent version comes later.

What "same-day teeth" actually means

It means leaving your implant surgery with a fixed temporary bridge already attached, not the final permanent restoration. The temporary is worn during healing; the permanent, custom-crafted piece comes later once your implants have fully integrated with the bone.

Who actually qualifies for immediate loading

It depends on implant stability confirmed during surgery and bone quality confirmed beforehand by your 3D scan. Not every case qualifies, and Dr. Rana will tell you directly if a staged approach, healing before any bridge is attached, is the safer option for your specific bone.

What the day of surgery looks like

Any remaining failing teeth are removed if needed, implants are placed, and a fixed temporary bridge is attached in the same visit. Most patients go home the same day with a functional, fixed set of teeth rather than a gap.

Healing and the final restoration

The temporary bridge is worn for the next few months while the implants fuse with your jawbone. Once healed, it's replaced with your final, permanent restoration, designed and milled to fit precisely.

Why our in-house lab matters here

Because both the temporary and final restorations are designed and crafted in our own in-house lab rather than outsourced, turnaround is faster and fit is checked directly by the team that made it, not relayed through a third party.

Eating during the healing period

The temporary bridge is functional, but we'll ask you to stick to softer foods while your implants integrate with the bone, gradually reintroducing firmer foods as healing progresses. It's a temporary adjustment, not a permanent diet change — once your final restoration is placed, it's built to handle normal chewing without restriction.

Common myths about same-day implants

Two myths come up constantly: that "same-day" means the permanent teeth are done in one visit (it means a fixed temporary, not the final piece), and that everyone automatically qualifies (implant stability and bone quality still have to check out first). We'd rather tell you honestly which is true for your case than let a marketing phrase set the wrong expectation.
