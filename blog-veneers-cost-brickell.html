how much do veneers cost if you live in brickell?

By Dr. Aakash Rana, DDS · Updated September 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: Porcelain veneers at Prana start at $999 per veneer, made in-house rather than sent to an outside lab. For Brickell residents, the practice is a short drive up Biscayne Blvd, with hours that fit a full workday.

If you work in Brickell and have been pricing out veneers, you've probably noticed how differently the cost gets explained from one office to the next, with numbers that seem to shift depending on who you ask and how the office structures its pricing behind the scenes. Here's a straightforward breakdown of what actually determines veneer cost, what it runs at Prana, and what the trip up from Brickell actually looks like on a normal workday.

What actually determines veneer cost

Veneer pricing depends mainly on how many veneers you're getting (a single tooth versus a full smile makeover of eight to ten), the material used, and — often overlooked — whether the practice designs and mills the veneers in-house or sends them to an outside lab that charges its own fee on top of everything else. All three factors combine to explain why one office's quote can look very different from another's for what sounds like the exact same treatment on paper. Knowing which of these is driving a given number makes it much easier to judge whether a quote is reasonable for what you're actually getting in return.

What veneers cost at Prana

Porcelain veneers at Prana start at $999 per veneer, with the total depending on how many teeth you're treating. Because the design and milling happen in Prana's in-house lab rather than an outsourced facility, there's no separate lab markup layered onto that per-veneer price — which is a meaningful part of why the published number is what it is rather than a placeholder that grows once you're already in the chair and further procedures get added on to the plan. It's a number you can plan an honest household budget around well ahead of time, not one that quietly shifts on you after your very first visit.

How many veneers most people actually get

Some patients want a single veneer to fix one chipped or discolored tooth; others are looking at a broader change across the visible front teeth, sometimes six to ten depending on how much of the smile shows when talking or laughing. There's no single right number — it depends on your specific teeth and what you're trying to change, which is why the per-veneer pricing structure makes it easier to plan for exactly what you need rather than being pushed into a fixed package that doesn't match your goals. Your dentist can walk you through all of the options at your consult once they've actually seen your teeth directly, in person, up close.

Financing if you're spreading out the cost

A full set of veneers is a bigger investment than a single tooth, and 0% financing is available from $167 a month on approved credit if you'd rather spread the cost into monthly payments instead of paying upfront in one lump sum. Checking your rate doesn't affect your credit, so it costs nothing to see what you'd qualify for before deciding how to pay for treatment or whether financing makes sense for you. It's a genuinely useful thing to check even before your consult, just to know roughly what's realistic for your household budget going in.
