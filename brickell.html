Brickell's pace and its dental needs

Brickell is Miami's banking and financial corridor — international bank offices, law firms, and high-rise condo towers around Brickell City Centre and Mary Brickell Village, most of them filled with people who moved there specifically to be within walking distance of work. Long, fixed office hours are the norm here, which doesn't leave much room in the middle of a weekday.

If your schedule only opens up before the market bell or after six, early-morning and evening appointment slots are built with exactly that in mind. And if you're newer to Miami and settled in Brickell without a dentist yet, getting started doesn't require transferring records from anywhere — a baseline exam and a scan is enough to begin.

The Miami River marks Brickell's northern edge, and Brickell Avenue itself runs along the bay past a run of bank headquarters that gave the area its reputation as a financial hub in the first place. Most of that stretch is set up for foot traffic between buildings rather than driving, which is part of why a car trip out of the neighborhood can feel like a bigger change of pace than the mileage alone suggests.
