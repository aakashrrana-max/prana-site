What cosmetic dentistry in Miami can include

Cosmetic dentistry covers any treatment aimed at improving how your smile looks, from small color changes to a full redesign. At Prana, that means veneers, professional whitening, Invisalign clear aligners, and crowns used cosmetically to rebuild damaged or worn teeth. Every plan starts with a real conversation about what bothers you about your smile and what a realistic result looks like — no upselling, no one-size-fits-all package.

Because Dr. Rana handles both your everyday dental health and your cosmetic work, treatment is planned around a healthy mouth first. That matters: cosmetic work lasts longer and looks better when it's built on healthy gums and a stable bite.

There's no single fixed package that fits everyone. Some patients want one chipped tooth fixed. Others want a complete redesign across their front teeth. We plan for both, and everything in between, with the same honest, written pricing before any work begins.

Veneers and full smile design

Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth to correct shape, color, gaps or wear, often across several teeth for a complete smile design. Because veneers and crowns can solve some of the same problems, patients often ask which one fits their situation — our veneers vs. crowns comparison walks through the difference in plain language. Veneers are a good fit for teeth that are structurally healthy but uneven in shape, size or color, and results are designed to look natural rather than uniform or overly white.

Professional teeth whitening

For patients who just want a brighter, more even shade without changing tooth shape, dentist-supervised teeth whitening is usually the simplest starting point. It's also a common complement to other cosmetic work, done before veneers or crowns so your new restorations are matched to your brightest natural shade. Whitening is dentist-supervised using professional-grade gels, which tends to give more even, longer-lasting results than store-bought kits, and it's safe for enamel when done under Dr. Rana's care.

Invisalign for a straighter smile

Crowded, gapped or crooked teeth affect how a smile looks even when every tooth is healthy. Invisalign clear aligners straighten teeth gradually and discreetly, and straightening is sometimes recommended before veneers so the final smile design has the best possible foundation. Treatment time varies by case, and Dr. Rana will give you a realistic timeline at your consult based on how much movement your teeth actually need.

Crowns for damaged or worn teeth

When a tooth is cracked, heavily worn, or already has a large filling, a crown can rebuild it while also improving its shape and color. Crowns made in our in-house lab are matched carefully to your surrounding teeth so cosmetic repairs blend in rather than stand out. Crowns can also be paired with whitening beforehand so the color of a new crown is planned around your brightest achievable shade, rather than matched to teeth that haven't been treated yet.

Replacing missing teeth

A missing tooth changes both function and appearance. Dental implants replace the root and the visible tooth, closing gaps in a way that's built to look and feel like the rest of your smile — an option worth discussing if a missing tooth is part of what you'd like to change. Because implants are placed and restored with the rest of your care in mind, they're planned alongside any other cosmetic work so your bite, gum health and appearance are all considered together, not treated as separate problems.
