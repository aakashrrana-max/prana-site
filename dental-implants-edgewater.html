Who single and multi-tooth implants suit

An implant is a small post set in the jawbone where a tooth used to be. Once the bone has healed around it, an abutment connects it to a custom crown. For one missing tooth, the teeth on either side don't have to be filed down the way they would for a traditional bridge. When two or three teeth in a row are gone, two implants can often hold a bridge that fills the whole gap.

Implants tend to work well for adults with healthy gums and enough bone, and age by itself is rarely what decides it. Your overall health counts for more, so bring a full list of medications, including blood thinners and any bone-density medicine, since they can change the plan. Smoking and poorly controlled diabetes can slow healing, and we'll talk about that honestly. The main dental implants page explains the treatment in more depth.

If most of the teeth in one jaw are failing, a full-arch approach may make more sense than replacing them one at a time; see All-on-4® in Edgewater for that route.

How treatment works, step by step

  • Your first visit, at no cost. An exam, a 3D scan and a smile preview, so you can see how the finished tooth could look.
  • Planning. If the bone needs a closer look, Dr. Rana may recommend X-rays or a CBCT scan, and he tells you why and what it costs before anything is taken.
  • Placement. Dr. Rana plans and treats your case himself. When a case needs grafting or care beyond what fits a general practice, he refers you to an oral surgeon.
  • Healing. The bone usually needs a few months to bond with the implant, depending on your bone and health.
  • Crown. Designed from a digital scan, made in our in-house lab, then fitted and adjusted to your bite.

Edgewater implant questions

Can I walk to Prana Dental from my building in Edgewater?

Many Edgewater residents can. The office is on Biscayne Blvd at NE 30th Street, so from most towers in the neighborhood it's a short walk or a few minutes by car. On a day with oral sedation, arrange for someone to bring you home.

Am I too old for a dental implant?

There's no set upper age limit for implants in adults. What weighs more is your general health, your medications and how much bone remains where the tooth was. The 3D scan at your first visit is where that conversation starts.

Can an implant replace an old bridge that's failing?

Often, yes. A bridge usually fails because one of the teeth holding it has decay or a crack. Depending on what the exam shows, the fix might be one or two implants, a new bridge or something else, and Dr. Rana will walk you through the trade-offs.

Do I need to bring X-rays from my previous dentist?

They help if you have them, but they aren't required. We begin with our own exam and 3D scan. If implant planning calls for more imaging, you hear the price before it's taken.
