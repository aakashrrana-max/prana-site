Life along the Edgewater bayfront

Edgewater is the run of high-rise condo towers along Biscayne Bay between Downtown and Midtown, with Margaret Pace Park serving as the neighborhood's main green space, ballfields, and waterfront running path. The area has filled in quickly over roughly the last fifteen years, and most of the buildings sit within a few blocks of the bay itself.

If you're one of the many residents who moved into a bayfront tower recently, getting set up with a local dentist is often just a matter of finding one close enough to fold into a normal week. A first visit here doesn't require records from a previous office — we start with our own exam and a 3D scan and build your chart from there.

Biscayne Blvd through Edgewater carries a steady mix of local traffic and buses rather than highway volume, so even during a busier stretch of the day the corridor tends to move; it's one reason a same-day or next-day booking is usually workable here.

The Edgewater waterfront

Margaret Pace Park runs along Biscayne Bay for several blocks and is the closest thing Edgewater has to a town square — a running path, ball fields, and a place plenty of nearby residents pass through on the way to or from our office. The park sits within easy walking distance of most of the neighborhood's towers.

Because so much of Edgewater is new construction from the last decade or two, a lot of residents are also new to the neighborhood and still building out their list of local providers — a dentist among them. Coming in without an established local relationship anywhere else in Miami isn't unusual here, and it doesn't slow anything down on our end.

The stretch of Biscayne Blvd running through Edgewater is lined mostly with newer mixed-use and residential towers rather than older low-rise buildings, a shift that's happened largely within the last couple of decades. That recent change is part of why the neighborhood still feels like it's filling in rather than fully settled.

Questions, answered

Where exactly is Prana Dental in Edgewater?

We're at 3050 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 703, right where Biscayne Boulevard meets NE 30th Street. The building is visible from either direction along Biscayne, so it's easy to spot on a first visit.

Is there parking at your Edgewater office?

Parking nearby is available, and a number of patients who live in the surrounding towers just walk over instead of driving.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Saturday, roughly eight to eight, and closed on Sunday. If a dental emergency comes up during those hours, walk-ins are welcome.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Most major dental insurance plans are accepted, and our team checks your benefits ahead of the visit. Whatever isn't covered can be spread out with 0% financing on approved credit.

Is the office bilingual?

Yes — every member of the team is comfortable working in English or Spanish, at no extra cost either way.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, an insurance card if you carry one, and a rundown of any medications you're currently taking. Older X-rays from a previous dentist are useful if you happen to have them, though not required.
