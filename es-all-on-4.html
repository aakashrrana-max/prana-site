Why timing matters

The jaw doesn't wait for you.

This is the part most people aren't told. Bone exists to hold teeth. Once a tooth is gone, the bone that held it starts to disappear — and it moves fastest right at the beginning.

30–60%of bone volume in the extraction site is lost within the first six months
3.8 mmaverage loss of ridge width in that same six months
1.24 mmaverage loss of ridge height

Figures from Hämmerle, Araújo & Simion, Clinical Oral Implants Research (2012), and Fickl et al., Journal of Clinical Periodontology (2008).

Two things follow from that. The first is practical: implants need bone to anchor into, so the longer an arch goes unrestored, the more likely grafting enters the conversation — which adds cost and months. The second is the thing people notice in the mirror without knowing why. As the ridge shrinks, the lower third of the face loses its support and shortens. It's the change long-term denture wearers see and describe as ageing faster than they expected.

None of this means you've missed your window. All-on-4 exists precisely because of bone loss — the rear implants are angled to reach dense bone that's still there, which is why grafting is avoided in most cases. But it does mean that the version of your jaw we can work with today is the best one you'll have. The free CT scan tells us exactly where you stand, and it costs you nothing to find out.

Questions

All-on-4, answered.

How much is All-on-4 in Miami?

From $9,999 per arch at Prana. Financing brings it to roughly $167/mo on approved credit. Nationally, full-arch implants commonly run $25,000–$30,000 per arch. Your exact plan is confirmed in writing at your free consult, before you commit to anything.

Will I leave without teeth?

No, you leave the same day with a fixed provisional set of teeth secured to your implants. There's no period of walking around without teeth and no removable temporary denture.

Does it hurt?

Treatment is done with sedation available and most patients are comfortable throughout and during recovery. The majority manage afterwards with over-the-counter pain relief for the first day or two.

Acrylic vs zirconia?

Acrylic is lighter on cost and easier to adjust. Zirconia is the most durable and the most natural-looking, and it resists staining. Ambas are milled in our own lab. We'll walk you through the tradeoff at your consult based on your bite, your budget, and how you want it to look.

Is it better than dentures?

For most people, yes. All-on-4 is fixed in place, so it doesn't slip, doesn't need adhesive, and doesn't come out at night. It also loads the jawbone the way natural teeth do, which slows the bone loss and facial collapse that long-term denture wearers experience.

What if I don't have enough bone?

The All-on-4 technique exists precisely because of bone loss — the rear implants are placed at an angle to reach dense bone and avoid grafting in most cases. Your free CT scan is how we know for certain. If you genuinely need more, we'll tell you honestly and lay out the options.

How long do they last?

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution and routinely last decades with proper maintenance. Your restoration is backed by our 5-year workmanship guarantee, and keeping your hygiene visits is what protects both the implants and the guarantee.

Does insurance cover any of it?

Some plans contribute toward extractions or a portion of the restoration, though full-arch implants are rarely covered in full. We'll review your benefits at the consult and show you the real out-of-pocket number, plus financing that fits it.

How long does the whole process take?

Surgery and same-day fixed teeth happen in one day. Your implants then fuse to the bone over roughly three to five months, during which you're wearing fixed teeth the entire time. Your final bridge is delivered after that.
