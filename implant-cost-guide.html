Does insurance cover it?

Dental insurance rarely covers implants beyond a small annual maximum, so most patients plan on financing or paying directly. We will tell you exactly what applies to you at your consultation.

How financing works

At Prana, full-arch treatment starts from $9,999 per arch, which is about $167 per month with 0% financing on approved credit. Approval is based on the patient's or a co-signer's credit, and your real monthly figure is confirmed in writing before any treatment.

Questions worth asking any provider

  • ✓ How many full-arch cases do you complete each year?
  • ✓ Who performs the surgery, and who makes the final teeth?
  • ✓ What exactly is included, and what is billed separately?
  • ✓ What happens if an adjustment or remake is needed?
  • ✓ Will I get the plan and the price in writing before I commit?
