Why it matters

Waiting can narrow your options

When teeth are missing, the jawbone that supported them can gradually shrink. Over time that can make implant treatment more complex, and in some cases it may mean extra procedures such as bone grafting.

A 3D scan is the only way to see where you stand today. That is why your consultation starts with one.
