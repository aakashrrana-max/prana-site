What we offer

Three treatments, one philosophy.

We treat conservatively and build gradually across visits. You can always add more later — taking it away is the hard part.

◇

Lip filler

Hyaluronic acid filler for definition, hydration, and balance. Shaped to your existing lip line rather than a template, and dosed so movement stays natural.

Typically 6–12 months
◇

Facial filler

Contour and support for the mid and lower face, including the perioral area, chin, and jawline. Especially relevant after tooth loss, where facial support changes over time.

Typically 9–18 months
◇

Wrinkle relaxing

Neuromodulator treatment to soften expression lines. Also used therapeutically for clenching and jaw tension, which is territory dentists know well.

Typically 3–4 months

Duration ranges are typical and vary by product, area treated, and individual metabolism. Suitability is assessed at your consultation.

Why here

A dentist knows this anatomy cold.

The area around the mouth is the hardest region on the face to inject well and the easiest to overfill. It is also the region a dentist works in every day of their career, on facial muscles, nerve pathways, and the underlying bone that gives the lower face its shape.

Anatomy, not guesswork

Facial and perioral anatomy is core dental training, and Dr. Rana works in this region daily.

It has to match your teeth

Lips frame teeth. If you've had veneers or a full-arch case, filler that ignores the smile line fights the work underneath it.

Conservative by default

We would rather bring you back to add a little than treat too much on day one.

Questions

Injectables, answered.

Why would a dentist do injectables?

Dentists work in the muscles and anatomy around the mouth every single day. The perioral region is the hardest area on the face to inject well and the easiest to overfill, and facial anatomy is central to dental training. If you've had cosmetic dental work, having the same person handle both means the filler and the smile line are designed together rather than in isolation.

How much does it cost?

Dosing varies for every patient, so we quote you in writing at your complimentary consultation before anything is scheduled. Monthly payment options are available.

How long do results last?

Wrinkle-relaxing treatment typically lasts three to four months. Hyaluronic acid filler typically lasts six to eighteen months depending on the product and the area treated. Individual results vary.

Will it look obvious?

Our default is conservative. We would rather start lower and bring you back at two weeks to add a little than overtreat on the first visit. You can always add more; taking it away is harder.

Does it hurt?

Most patients describe it as brief and very manageable. We use topical numbing where appropriate and work at your pace.

Is there downtime?

Most people return to normal activity the same day. Some swelling or bruising is possible, particularly with lip filler, and we'll tell you exactly what to expect and how to plan around anything you have coming up.

Who isn't a candidate?

Injectables aren't appropriate for everyone. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, certain medical conditions and medications, and some skin infections all rule treatment out. This is exactly what the consultation is for, and you must be 18 or older.
