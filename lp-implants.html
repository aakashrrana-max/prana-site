What $1,999 includes

Three parts. One price.

Part 1

The implant

A small titanium post that takes the place of the tooth root. Your bone grows around it and holds it tight.

Part 2

The abutment

The connector that sits on top of the implant and holds your new tooth in place.

Part 3

Zirconia crown

Your new tooth. Strong, natural-looking, and shade-matched to your smile in our own lab.

  • Taking out a damaged tooth (extraction)

  • Sedation, if you'd like to be more relaxed
  • Treating gum disease or other problems first

Questions

Straight answers

Is $1,999 the full price?

$1,999 covers the implant, the abutment and a zirconia crown for one tooth. If you need extra care first, like an extraction or bone graft, we tell you the cost in writing before you agree to anything.

What other procedures might I need?

Some people need the old tooth taken out, a bone graft, or gum treatment first. Some choose sedation. Your exam and 3D scan show what you need. Many people need nothing extra.

Does it hurt?

Most patients are surprised how comfortable it is. Your mouth is numbed, oral sedation is available, and we go at your pace.

How long does it take?

It depends on your bone and gums. The implant needs time to heal into your bone before the final crown goes on. We give you your own timeline in your written plan.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. We work with patient-financing partners such as CareCredit, Cherry and LendingClub, including 0% options on approved credit. We'll go over your choices at the consult.

Am I a candidate?

Most adults with healthy gums and enough bone can get an implant. Even if you've been told "no" before, a free exam and 3D scan is the only way to know for sure.

How long do implants last?

With good care, implants can last decades. We back our workmanship with a 5-year guarantee.*
