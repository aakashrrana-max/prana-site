Before you decide

The honest trade-offs.

All-on-4 changes a lot of lives, but it is a big decision. You should hear the downsides from us first.

  • It is surgery. Swelling and soreness for several days are normal, and healing takes months.
  • Extractions can't be undone. If some of your teeth can be saved, we will tell you.
  • Soft foods at first while the implants bond to the bone.
  • Daily cleaning takes effort. A water flosser and regular maintenance visits are part of life with it.
  • Not everyone qualifies for same-day teeth. Thin or soft bone may need grafting or a staged plan. Complex cases are referred to an oral surgeon.
  • Implants can fail. It isn't common, but smoking, uncontrolled diabetes and poor cleaning raise the risk.
Questions

All-on-4, answered.

Am I a candidate?

It depends on your health, your bone and your remaining teeth, which is exactly what the free exam and 3D scan check. If All-on-4 isn't the best fit, Dr. Rana will explain the other options, including a different number of implants or a staged plan, and refer you to a specialist when that's the right call.

Will I leave without teeth?

Most All-on-4 plans aim for a fixed provisional set of teeth on the same day as the implants. That depends on how firmly the implants hold. If your bone needs a different approach, you'll know before treatment day, not on the day itself.

Does it hurt?

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, and oral sedation is available if you'd like it. Swelling and soreness for several days afterward are normal. Dr. Rana will go over pain control with you before treatment.

What does the free first visit include?

An exam, a 3D intraoral scan and a digital smile preview. It does not include a cleaning, X-rays or any treatment. You leave with your options and a written price, with no obligation.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. 0% financing is available on approved credit through CareCredit, Cherry and LendingClub. For example, $9,999 over 60 months at 0% APR is about $167 a month. Terms vary by lender, and we go over your options at your first visit.

Will insurance cover it?

Some plans contribute toward extractions or part of the restoration, but full-arch implants are rarely covered in full. We accept most major dental insurance, check your benefits for you and show you the real out-of-pocket number.

How long does it take?

Implants and provisional teeth are placed on one treatment day. The implants then bond to the bone over roughly three to five months, and your final arch is fitted after that.
