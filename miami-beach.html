Crossing over from Miami Beach

From Miami Beach, I-195 — the Julia Tuttle Causeway — is the direct link west into Edgewater, landing you close to Biscayne Blvd; the crossing itself usually takes about fifteen minutes depending on traffic. Our office is at 3050 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 703, a short distance off the causeway.

Parking nearby is available on arrival. Send a note on WhatsApp as you're crossing and we'll be ready for you.

The causeway tends to slow down during peak commute hours in either direction, so a weekday mid-morning or early-afternoon appointment usually makes for a smoother ride.

Once you're off the causeway on the Edgewater side, the last stretch to our office is a short run down Biscayne Blvd itself — no additional bridges or merges, just the boulevard the rest of the way.

A neighborhood that runs on visitors

Miami Beach runs on tourism and hospitality — hotels, restaurants, and the South Beach Art Deco Historic District draw both year-round residents and a large seasonal population, alongside condo owners who split their year between the Beach and somewhere else. That mix means schedules across the island vary widely, from fixed shift work to flexible remote days.

If your work puts you on camera or in front of clients, appointments can be planned around a shoot date or an event on your calendar. And for anyone only on the Beach part of the year, Invisalign check-ins or a longer treatment plan can be scheduled around the weeks you're actually in town rather than a fixed weekly visit.

Much of the island runs later than a typical Miami neighborhood — restaurants, shops, and hotel schedules on the Beach tend to stretch well past a standard workday, and quite a few residents keep hours that follow suit. That's part of why a late-afternoon or evening appointment tends to be an easy ask here.

Digital previews, made in-house

Cosmetic cases start with a digital 3D scan, so you get a preview of the outcome before committing to treatment — handy if you're thinking about how your smile reads on camera. Because veneers and crowns are made in our own lab instead of being sent out, that timeline doesn't depend on an outside courier.

Dr. Aakash Rana, DDS — a general dentist who studied at NYU College of Dentistry — stays closely involved in every cosmetic case that comes through. Cosmetic and implant treatment also comes with a 5-year workmanship guarantee; the details are on our guarantee page.

When we're open

We're open every day but Sunday, Monday through Saturday, from 8 in the morning to 8 at night. Most major dental insurance is accepted, and 0% financing on approved credit is an option for whatever isn't covered.

A new-patient visit is free — an exam, a 3D scan, and a written plan, detailed on our new patient page. Our team works comfortably in English and Spanish, which comes up often given how international the Beach's mix of residents and visitors tends to be.

An island shaped by tourism

South Beach's Art Deco Historic District is one of the largest concentrations of Art Deco architecture anywhere, and it's part of what keeps Miami Beach a year-round draw rather than a purely seasonal one. Ocean Drive and Lincoln Road sit at the center of that, with a steady flow of both residents and visitors moving through year round.

That visitor traffic also means Miami Beach's own streets can run slower than the causeway itself, particularly near the entertainment district on a weekend evening — something worth factoring in if you're timing a trip over to Edgewater around your own schedule on the island side.

North Beach and the mid-Beach hotel corridor tend to run a bit quieter than South Beach proper, which can make the drive to the Julia Tuttle on-ramp a little more predictable if you're starting from farther up the island rather than down near Ocean Drive.
