The in-between stage at work

Temporaries

When teeth are prepared, you usually wear temporary veneers or a mock-up while the porcelain is made in our lab. Most people find them presentable for meetings, but they can feel a little thicker and are less polished than the final result. Plan the preparation visit before a lighter week rather than the night before a pitch.

Speech

Some people notice a slight change in certain sounds, such as "s" or "f", for a few days while the tongue adjusts to new edges. It usually settles quickly. If it doesn't, the shape can often be refined at a follow-up.

Honest candidacy, prep and upkeep

Veneers are a good fit for many adults whose teeth and gums are healthy and whose concerns are mainly cosmetic. They are a poor fit if there is untreated decay or gum disease, and grinding from stress can shorten their life unless it is managed, often with a night guard. A tooth that is heavily filled or cracked may need a crown instead.

Traditional porcelain veneers usually involve removing a thin layer of enamel, which can't be undone. Minimal-prep veneers may be possible when teeth are small or slightly behind the arch, but that depends on your scan and exam, not on a preference. Lifespan varies from person to person; porcelain often lasts many years with daily brushing and flossing, regular cleanings, and not chewing on pens during long calls.

Questions, answered

Can I do a veneer consult on my lunch break from Downtown Miami?

Often, yes. The drive north on Biscayne Blvd is usually 10 to 15 minutes depending on traffic, and the free first visit is an exam, 3D scan and smile preview with no treatment that day. If your lunch hour is short, an early-morning or after-work slot may be less rushed.

Will coworkers notice my temporary veneers?

Most people find temporaries look acceptable for everyday work, though they are not as refined as the finished porcelain. They can feel slightly bulky at first. Scheduling the preparation visit ahead of a quieter week helps.

Will veneers change how I sound in meetings?

Some people notice a small change in sounds like s or f for a few days after new veneers or temporaries. For many, the tongue adapts quickly. If something still feels off, the edges can often be adjusted.
