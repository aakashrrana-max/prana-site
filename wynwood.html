Wynwood's working hours

Wynwood is Miami's arts and warehouse district — Wynwood Walls, working galleries, and a growing cluster of breweries and restaurants that keep the neighborhood busy well past a typical workday. A lot of the people who work here, from gallery staff to line cooks, keep schedules that don't match a standard nine-to-five.

If your shift runs late or shifts week to week, mention it when you book and we'll work around it rather than pushing you into a standard weekday slot. For anyone newer to the neighborhood's growing residential side who still needs to find a dentist, a free consultation is a simple place to begin.

Because so many Wynwood businesses are small and independently run, a lot of the people working there don't have employer dental coverage built in the way a larger office might — worth keeping in mind, since most major individual dental insurance plans are still accepted here.

From warehouses to murals

Wynwood's outdoor murals grew out of a district that was mostly garment-district and warehouse buildings, and that industrial layout is still visible even as galleries, breweries, and shops have filled the same blocks in. It's a neighborhood built for walking between spots once you're parked, rather than driving from block to block.

That same layout means Wynwood doesn't sit directly on a Metrorail or Metromover line, so most people moving between Wynwood and Edgewater are either driving, biking, or taking a rideshare rather than transit.

The district's breweries and restaurants add a second wave of activity most evenings, on top of the daytime gallery crowd, which keeps parking and street traffic tighter later in the day than during regular business hours.

Bikes and scooters are a common way to get around inside Wynwood itself once you've parked once, given how tight the blocks are, though for the trip out to Edgewater most people switch back to a car or a rideshare rather than riding the whole way.
